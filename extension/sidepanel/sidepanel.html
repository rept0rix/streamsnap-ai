How to Get Your Amazon Affiliate Tag

  1. Go to affiliate-program.amazon.com ↗ and click Sign Up.
  2. Log in with your Amazon account and enter your channel link (YouTube / Twitch / TikTok).
  3. Choose your Preferred Store ID (e.g. yourname-20).
  4. Copy your Store ID and paste it in the box below to earn 100% of your affiliate commissions!

All product links and cart additions will use this tag for commission attribution.
