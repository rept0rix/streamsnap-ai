Privacy Policy & Transparency

StreamSnap AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

StreamSnap AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides a visual product discovery application available on mobile devices (Android and iOS) and as a web extension. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle and protect data when you use the StreamSnap AI mobile application and associated services.

Key Principle: StreamSnap AI is designed to respect your media and device privacy. We do not sell your personal data or visual media. Images scanned with your camera or selected from your gallery are used exclusively for real-time product matching.

1. Permissions and Information We Collect

A. Device Permissions

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Used only when you choose to scan a product, stream, or video feed live. The camera stream is used in real-time to analyze and extract product features for matching. We do not store live video feeds.
  • Photos and Storage Permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used when you import or share a screenshot or image to search for products. Only the specific image you deliberately select or share with StreamSnap AI is accessed.

B. Account and Identity Data

  • Google Sign-In & Authentication: If you sign in, we receive basic authentication profile information from Google (such as your name, email address, and profile picture). This is used solely to identify your account, sync your saved items, and manage subscription/usage quotas.
  • Anonymous Usage: If you use features without logging in, device-specific anonymous identifiers may be used solely for session rate-limiting.

C. Scanned Images & Visual Data

  • Images or crops submitted for recognition are transmitted securely via encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS) to our backend processing pipeline.
  • Image data is processed ephemerally by computer vision and artificial intelligence algorithms (including Google Cloud Vision and Gemini AI) solely to recognize products.
  • We do not build facial recognition profiles or biometric databases.

2. How We Use the Information

We use collected data strictly to:

  • Identify products from your photos or camera scans and return matching search results.
  • Provide you with affiliate purchase links to relevant marketplaces (e.g., Amazon).
  • Maintain your account, search history, and saved bookmarks (accessible only to you).
  • Prevent abuse, protect against fraud, and enforce service quotas.

3. Third-Party Services and Subprocessors

We work with trusted industry service providers who comply with stringent security standards:

  • Google Cloud & Gemini AI: For visual analysis and machine learning product identification.
  • Cloudflare: For secure infrastructure hosting, DDoS protection, and edge API routing.
  • Amazon Associates / Marketplaces: When you tap on product links, you are directed to the respective marketplace. Marketplaces govern your shopping experience under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your account profile and search history for as long as your account remains active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at naor@streamsnap.online or through the in-app settings. Requests are processed within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

StreamSnap AI is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

We implement standard industry-level technical and administrative security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect service updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
