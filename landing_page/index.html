WHY IT FINDS THINGS A SEARCH BOX CAN'T

It doesn't describe the object. It searches with the image.

Most "AI shopping" tools

Look at an image, write a description, search that description.

olive drop-shoulder fleece → "green hoodie" → 40,000 results

The same dead end you'd reach by hand — just reached faster.

StreamSnap

Sends the crop itself to a reverse image search across the retail web.

the actual crop → visual match → the thing you pointed at

Matches on shape, cut, and logo placement — not on adjectives.

And the part that matters most: we only link to listings we can confirm are real.

If StreamSnap resolves your crop to a specific verified product, you get a direct link to that product page. If it can't confirm the exact listing, it says so — the card is marked Visual match, and it takes you to a filtered search instead of a product page.

We would rather tell you "this is our best visual guess" than send you to a dead product page and let you find out yourself.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The things you're probably wondering

"Does it actually work, or is it AI that guesses?"

It searches by image, not by description, and it tells you when it isn't sure. A card marked Visual match means a visual similarity, not a confirmed listing. You'll always know which one you're looking at.

"Is it watching everything I browse?"

No. It runs on five streaming domains and nowhere else — not your bank, not your email. Nothing is analysed unless you press a button. The crop you select is the only thing that leaves your browser, and it's deleted the moment the search comes back.

"Do I need to sign up?"

Not to try it. You get scans immediately on install. An account only matters when you want history synced across devices, or you're using it heavily.

"Do I need an API key or technical setup?"

No. Install and use it.

"What's the catch if it's free?"

When you buy through a link, we may earn an affiliate commission from the retailer. You pay the same price either way. That's the entire business model, and now you know it.

"What if it doesn't find what I'm looking for?"

Sometimes it won't — an unbranded or custom item may have no listing anywhere. You'll get a search for the closest match rather than a fake result.

FAQ

Anything else?

Which sites does it work on?+

YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Facebook and Kick. It doesn't load on any other site.

Which browsers?+

Chrome, and Chromium-based browsers like Edge, Brave and Opera.

Does it slow down the stream?+

No. It only works when you ask it to, and a repeated scan of an unchanged scene reuses the previous result instead of running again.

What happens to the images?+

The crop you select is sent to be matched, then deleted in the same request. It isn't stored and it isn't used for training. Product results are cached; images never are.

Can I use it for things that aren't on Amazon?+

Yes. When something isn't available on Amazon, other retailers can be shown instead.

Is my browsing history collected?+

No. The extension only runs on the five streaming domains, and it never sends page URLs or browsing history anywhere.

How do you make money?+
