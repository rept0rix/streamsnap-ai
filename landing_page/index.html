Frequently Asked Questions

Which streaming platforms are supported?+

YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Kick, and Facebook Live.

Do I need an Amazon Associates ID to use StreamSnap?+

No! If you are a viewer or shopper, you do not need any Amazon tag. An Amazon tag is only required if you are a streamer or creator who wants to collect affiliate commissions on your own streams.

How do I sign in?+

You can sign in with 1-click Google Sign-In directly inside the Chrome Extension setup tab, or use your own free Gemini API key.

Does it slow down the stream?+

Not at all. StreamSnap captures a single lightweight frame only when you click or trigger a scan.

Is it free?+

Yes, 100% free to install from the Chrome Web Store with no subscriptions or hidden charges.
