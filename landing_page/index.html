FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Is StreamSnap AI really free to use? +

Yes! StreamSnap AI is 100% free and open-source. You can install it on Chrome, Brave, Edge, or Opera. You can connect your free Google Gemini API key to run unlimited visual searches.

How does the 1-Click Amazon Cart work? +

When verified products are detected, StreamSnap AI adds them to your local session cart. When you click "Checkout Cart", it uses Amazon's official Remote Cart gateway to stage all items into your personal Amazon basket in a single click.

Is my browsing history or video stream private? +

Absolutely. StreamSnap AI only captures a video crop when you explicitly click Scan or Snip. It contains zero trackers, no telemetry, and never sells or transmits personal data. Review our Privacy Policy.

Can creators earn commissions from their viewers? +

Yes! Streamers can enter their Amazon Associate tracking tag in the Setup tab. Any viewer shopping their stream gear routes through their affiliate ID, earning them up to 10% commission on qualifying sales.
