# 📊 StreamSnap AI — Comprehensive Business Plan & 3-Year Financial Model

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**StreamSnap AI** is a real-time computer vision commerce platform that turns any video stream into an interactive, 1-Click Amazon shopping experience.

---

## 2. Revenue Streams (Monetization Architecture)

### 1. Amazon Associates Multi-Item Commission (Primary)
- **Cookie Window:** Amazon 24-hour cart cookie + 30-day multi-item cart accumulation.
- **Average Take Rate:** 4.5% across Apparel (7%), Tech/Audio (3%), Home/Sports (5%).
- **Projected Revenue per Active User:** \$1.20 – \$3.40 / month.

### 2. Creator Affiliate RevShare Platform (B2B2C)
- Streamers sign up and connect their customized sub-affiliate ID.
- StreamSnap AI splits affiliate commissions **60% to Creator / 40% to StreamSnap AI**.
- Creates viral organic distribution as top streamers instruct their audiences to install StreamSnap.

### 3. StreamSnap PRO Subscription (\$4.99 / month)
- Power user tier: Unlimited automated background scanning (every 5 seconds), price drop alerts on stream items, and priority Gemini Ultra multi-angle vision models.

### 4. Amazon Prime Bounty & Sponsorship
- \$3.00 – \$5.00 bounty for every non-Prime user converted during stream checkout.

---

## 3. Zero-Cost Token Architecture & Unit Economics

| Cost Component | Traditional Vision LLM | StreamSnap Zero-Cost Architecture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **API Calls per 10min Stream** | 60 calls (\$0.12) | **2–4 calls (\$0.004)** |
| **Frame Filter Ratio** | 0% (All frames sent) | **88% filtered locally via Canvas Diff & pHash** |
| **Cost per 1,000 Scans** | \$2.00 | **\$0.16 (Over 90% Cost Reduction)** |
| **Gross Margin** | ~65% | **94.8% (Hyper-Scalable)** |
