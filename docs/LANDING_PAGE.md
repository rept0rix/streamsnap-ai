## 4. Solution Section

**StreamSnap is a Chrome extension that turns anything on a live stream into a link.**

Install it, open a stream, and three buttons appear on the video player.

**Scan Frame** reads the whole frame and boxes every product it finds.
**Snip Box** lets you drag a box around one specific item.
**Click-to-Find** lets you just click the object while the video plays.

Whichever you use, the matching products appear in a side panel next to the video. Add to cart, or save it for later.

Works on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Facebook and Kick.

## 5. Mechanism Section

**Why this finds things a search box can't.**

Most "AI shopping" tools do the same thing you'd do by hand, just faster: they look at an image, write a description, and search that description. So a specific olive drop-shoulder fleece becomes "green hoodie", and you get 40,000 results — the same dead end, reached quicker.

StreamSnap doesn't describe the object. **It searches with the image itself.**

The crop you select goes to a reverse image search across the whole retail web, which matches on the actual visual — the shape of the earcup, the cut of the sleeve, the logo placement — and comes back with products that genuinely look like the thing you pointed at.

**And here's the part that matters most: we only link to listings we can confirm are real.**

If StreamSnap resolves your crop to a specific verified product, you get a direct link to that product page. If it can't confirm the exact listing, it says so — the card is marked **Visual match**, and it takes you to a filtered search instead of a product page.

We would rather tell you "this is our best visual guess" than send you to a dead Amazon page and let you find out yourself. Every competing tool we looked at does the second thing.

## 8. Objection Handling

**"Does it actually work, or is it AI that guesses?"**
It searches by image, not by description, and it will tell you when it isn't sure. A card marked *Visual match* means exactly that — a visual similarity, not a confirmed listing. You'll always know which one you're looking at.

**"Is it watching everything I browse?"**
No. It runs on five streaming domains and nowhere else — not your bank, not your email. Nothing is analysed unless you press a button. The crop you select is the only thing that ever leaves your browser, and it's deleted the moment the search comes back.

**"Do I need to sign up?"**
Not to try it. You get scans immediately on install. An account only comes in when you want your history synced across devices or you're using it heavily.

**"Do I need an API key or any technical setup?"**
No. Install and use it.

**"What's the catch if it's free?"**
When you buy through a link, we may earn an affiliate commission from the retailer. You pay the same price either way. That's the entire business model, and now you know it.

**"What if it doesn't find what I'm looking for?"**
Sometimes it won't — an unbranded item or a custom piece may have no listing anywhere. You'll get a search for the closest match rather than a fake result.

## 12. FAQ

**Which sites does it work on?**
YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Facebook and Kick. It doesn't load on any other site.

**Which browsers?**
Chrome, and Chromium-based browsers like Edge, Brave and Opera.

**Does it slow down the stream?**
No. It only does work when you ask it to, and repeated scans of an unchanged scene reuse the previous result instead of running again.

**What happens to the images?**
The crop you select is sent to be matched, then deleted in the same request. It isn't stored, and it isn't used for training. Product results are cached; images never are.

**Can I use it for things that aren't on Amazon?**
Yes. When something isn't available on Amazon, other retailers can be shown instead.

**Is my browsing history collected?**
No. The extension only runs on the five streaming domains, and it never sends page URLs or browsing history anywhere.

**Is it available now?**
It's in review for the Chrome Web Store. Join the list and you'll get a link the day it's live — or install it manually today if you'd rather not wait.
